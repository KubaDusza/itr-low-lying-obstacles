## Evaluation and key current results

Primary shared evaluation: object coverage (at least 50% of supplied reference pixels covered by union of predictions), plus adapted **SegmentMeIfYouCan** component metrics. Published adjusted overlap/component precision tolerates useful splits and merges, penalizes excess foreground. Mean component F1 uses 11 thresholds .25,.30,...,.75. Supplied IDs replace binary GT connected components; no small-target filtering; one uncertain four-pixel fragment ignored for components/boxes. Native boxes are filled and unioned before shared scoring.

Strict native box AP is separate; standard confidence-ordered one-to-one matches can penalize granularity. Do not replace primary evaluation with box counts or an invented heuristic. `code/hazard_metrics.py` has 11 safety checks; prior differential audit tested 50 random cases against official implementation. Bibliography cites Chan et al., NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks 2021. Do not invent a citation count.
