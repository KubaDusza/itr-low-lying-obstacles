# Result Report rubric (Canvas, 15 points)

Submitted as one PDF in IEEE format, appended to the bibliography survey and methodology (assignments 1 and 2).

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Follows IEEE template | 1 |
| Results section present, length consistent with a conference paper (2 to 4 columns) | 1 |
| Conclusion or future work section present | 1 |
| Results demonstrate the effectiveness of the method; they evaluate the method | 2 |
| Experiments on which the results are computed are clearly described | 2 |
| Results supported by graphs and/or tables | 1 |
| Figure/table captions help understanding | 2 |
| All figures/tables referred to in the text | 1 |
| All figures/tables clear, elements explained | 1 |
| Each figure/table clearly supports a result | 2 |
| Conclusion/future work highlights strengths/limitations and proposes a path for future projects | 1 |
